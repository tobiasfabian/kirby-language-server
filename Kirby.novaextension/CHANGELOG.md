## 0.1.0

- Completion for `snippet('…')`, `collection('…')` and `t('…')`/`tt('…')`/`tc('…')`, also `kirby.collection("…")` in blueprint queries, with each entry's origin, variables, signature or text
- Completion for `type:` in blueprints: field types, and section types under `sections:` in Kirby 4 and 5
- Command Open Snippet, Collection or Translation (⌃⌥⌘O)
- Warnings for snippets, collections and translation keys that don't exist
